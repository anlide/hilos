You may request deletion of your account. Analytics is an exception to full erasure: after deletion, the numeric account number, events attributed to it, and recorded network addresses remain. Those addresses may allow a person to be recognized again.

The raw analytics currently has no automatic deletion period. It remains until a retention and deletion mechanism is introduced. Your data copy includes events attributed to your account, including accounts folded into it; events of a different person using the same browser are not treated as yours.
